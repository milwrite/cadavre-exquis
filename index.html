“The exquisite corpse will drink the new wine.” the game’s first sentence, Paris, 1925

Exquisite Corpse is a parlor game: each player writes a few words on a sheet, folds it to hide what came before, and passes it on. This version of the game seats a language model at the table. Submit one to five words per line for every turn you play, then hand off the poem to the next in line for them to follow up. Rinse and repeat until a human player chooses to end the game and reveal the poem, with close reading analysis to accompany it.
